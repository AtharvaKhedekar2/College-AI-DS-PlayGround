      How time is estimated

      • Theory modules: each subject's self-learning hours from the syllabus, shared across its modules by lecture hours, in sessions of up to 1 hour.
      • Revision: 30 minutes per theory module, after first study.
      • IAT revision: 45 minutes per theory subject in the three days before a test you've dated.
      • Tutorial sheet: 45 minutes. Lab experiment write-up: 30 minutes.
      • Nirman Lab step or monthly review: 1 hour. Workshop or skills module: 30 minutes.
      • Assignment: 1 hour. Term work submission: 45 minutes. Viva prep, mini project or quiz: 2 hours.

      Time in class isn't scheduled, only your own study. Only the theory estimate comes from the syllabus; the others are starting points you can change on any task.

      Due dates: the syllabus sets 15 weeks of teaching but no dates, so these are targets counted from your semester start. Assignments fall in weeks 6 and 11, mini projects and quizzes in week 13, term work in week 15 and viva prep in week 16.
